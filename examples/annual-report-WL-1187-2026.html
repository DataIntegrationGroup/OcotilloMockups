How to Read This Report

MCL (Maximum Contaminant Level) — an enforceable federal health-based limit for public water systems. Private wells are not regulated, but the limit is the best available yardstick.

SMCL (Secondary MCL) — a non-health limit covering taste, odor, color, and staining. Exceeding it is a nuisance, not a health risk.

ND (Not Detected) — below what the instrument can measure. The value shown is the detection limit, not the concentration.

mg/L — milligrams per liter, roughly one part per million.

Percentile — "71st percentile" means 71% of nearby wells had a lower result than yours.

Ion balance — a laboratory check that the positive and negative ions add up. A passing balance means the analysis is internally consistent.

Limitations — results describe the water on the day it was sampled, at the point it was sampled. Water quality changes with season, pumping, and household plumbing. This report does not certify water as safe to drink.
